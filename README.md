**📖 Executive Summary**

In today's rapidly evolving digital world, individuals accumulate valuable digital assets such as financial accounts, cryptocurrency wallets, cloud storage, intellectual property, social media accounts, AI assistants, digital subscriptions, and personal documents. Despite their increasing value, India currently lacks a standardized framework to securely manage and facilitate the inheritance of these digital assets after the owner's death or incapacity.

Digital Will AI is an AI-powered Digital Legacy Management Platform specifically designed for the Indian legal and cybersecurity ecosystem. Instead of replacing existing succession laws, it functions as a secure intermediary that enables legally auditable and transparent digital inheritance through a multi-stage verification process.

The platform combines Artificial Intelligence, eKYC verification, AI-powered fraud detection, AES-256 encryption, WebAuthn authentication, cryptographic audit logging, and DPDP-compliant privacy controls to ensure that digital assets are transferred only after successful identity verification, legal validation, nominee authentication, and mandatory contest periods.

By integrating modern cybersecurity principles with legal compliance and intelligent automation, Digital Will AI aims to establish a trusted, secure, and scalable foundation for managing India's future digital inheritance ecosystem.
